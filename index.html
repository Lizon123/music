How did it all start?

This site was born from a single random melody. One evening I was walking through the city, an old track that I hadn’t listened to in years was playing in my headphones. Suddenly the whole world around me changed: the lights lit up brighter, the wind played in my hair, and my heart seemed to beat to the rhythm of the music. I realized: music is a time machine. It takes us back to the best moments of life, helps us feel what is difficult to express in words, and even becomes the soundtrack to our future stories. That’s when the idea came: to create a space where every sound would be not just a track, but a part of you.
